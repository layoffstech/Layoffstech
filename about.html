About Mamta Media

Mamta Media is a general-interest online magazine that shares genuine, easy-to-read information on the topics that matter in everyday life.

Who we are

Mamta Media is built and run by the Mamta Media Team. We are a group of writers who each bring their own interests and experience. Because our authors are different people, every article shows the name of the writer who prepared it, so you always know who is behind the story.

What we write about

We publish across eight categories, so there is something for every reader:

Technology for gadgets, apps, AI and online safety. Business for money, careers and small-business ideas. Entertainment for movies, shows and music. Lifestyle for home, habits and everyday tips. Health for wellness and fitness basics. Sports for games, players and big events. Education for study help, exams and learning. Travel for places, planning and budget trips.

How we work

Genuine information first. We research every topic using trusted sources such as official websites, government and education bodies, and reputable publications, and we link to our main sources inside the article where it helps.

Simple, clear writing. We explain things in plain English so that anyone can understand them, whether you are a student, a professional or simply curious.

Original content. Our articles are written by our own team. We do not copy content from other websites.

Kept up to date. Information changes over time. When it does, we update our articles so they stay useful.

Corrections

We work hard to get every fact right, but if you spot a mistake, please tell us. We will check it, fix it and update the article.

Important note

Our content is for general information and learning. It is not a replacement for advice from a qualified doctor, lawyer or financial expert. Please read our Disclaimer and Privacy Policy for more details.
